Understand your market map.

Build a selection of crypto assets and xStocks, read their returns and check the data behind them. Orbit is a read-only monitoring tool, with no account or wallet connection.

01

Build your watchlist

From the home page, choose Build my watchlist. Search for a name or symbol, then use the All assets, Crypto or xStocks filters. Add the assets you want to follow and open your selection.

Your watchlist can hold up to 50 assets across both classes. Use the “+” button to manage them later. The Crypto view shows up to 100 assets; the xStocks view shows up to 50, ranked by token market cap. The searchable catalog also includes collected assets outside these maps.

In settings, export your watchlist as JSON to keep a copy or move it to another browser. Import lets you merge a selection or replace it. Both operations stay on your device.

02

Read the map

Choose a period: 1H, 24H, 7D or 30D. Color represents the price return over that period: green for a rise, red for a fall. A neutral tile can indicate unusable or older data; open its sheet to check.

The Size setting changes tile area: performance by default, market cap or volume. A large tile does not necessarily represent a large company or deep liquidity. Changing this setting does not change the return period.

Select a tile to see its price in US dollars, returns and available metrics. The seven-day chart appears only when the source supplies enough points. Use arrow keys to move between tiles, Enter to open a sheet and Escape to close it.

03

What xStocks represent

According to the issuer’s documentation, xStocks are tokenized tracker certificates providing economic exposure to a stock or ETF. They do not confer shareholder rights, including voting rights. Read the issuer’s FAQ.

Orbit displays the token market.

Price, market cap and volume describe the token tracked by CoinGecko. Token market cap is not the underlying company’s equity value. Missing metrics remain unavailable.

Secondary-market token prices depend on supply and demand; trading hours and liquidity depend on the venue. Orbit does not substitute the underlying stock price for a missing token quote. See the issuer’s explanation of prices and trading hours.

xStocks have a dedicated view and can join your crypto watchlist. They are excluded from the crypto statistical reference, anomalies, divergences and Bitcoin comparisons. Their sheets open directly on market data. Orbit does not support buying, selling or transferring tokens.

04

Check sources and freshness

CoinGecko

Prices, returns, volumes, market caps and charts when supplied. The xStocks feed is collected separately from the crypto feed.

LunarCrush

Crypto social context, when available. Symbol matches remain indicative and do not produce market signals.

FRED

Macroeconomic context, when available. Observation dates are separate from collection times.

The Data sources panel shows each feed’s state and dates. Collection time is when the collector retrieved data. A price timestamp, when supplied, comes from the provider: receiving a recent file does not make every quote current.

Market collection older than three minutes, or with unknown age, disables the relevant signals. Supplied price timestamps are checked separately. If the xStocks fetch fails, the last real quotes can remain visible with their original timestamps and a degraded status. No replacement values are invented.

Crypto and xStocks collection each use a minimum 60-second interval by default, with a timer tick roughly every 30–45 seconds. The browser reads the shared file every 30–45 seconds. Server overrides or provider backoff may lengthen these intervals. Quote age is separate: xStocks quotes aged 3–10 minutes are labelled delayed; older quotes or those without a valid timestamp use neutral tiles. These are display rules, not a real-time guarantee. Collection failures are reported separately and original timestamps are preserved.

The page periodically reads a shared snapshot. Refreshing the browser does not trigger provider API requests. It guarantees neither a new trade nor an executable price. CoinGecko market-data contract.

05

Understand crypto signals

The reference contains usable crypto assets in the snapshot, including stablecoins, and requires at least 20 valid observations. It is not limited to your watchlist and excludes xStocks. The median is the middle return; rising / unchanged / falling counts give each asset equal weight.

Price anomaly
The absolute z-score exceeds 2: the return is more than two population standard deviations from the reference mean.
Activity anomaly
The z-score exceeds 2 for log10(1 + 1000 × 24h volume / market cap). Volume alone does not establish net buying.
Divergence
In the 24H view only, the absolute gap between price and activity z-scores exceeds 1.2.
Bitcoin-relative return
100 × ((100 + asset return in %) / (100 + BTC return in %) − 1). Dated prices must be current and at most one minute apart. Without compatible data, the comparison remains unavailable.
Gap to the median
Asset return minus median return, expressed in percentage points.

These calculations compare assets at one moment, not an asset against its own past. Outliers and reference composition affect the results. A zero count is not a measure of low risk; no score is a probability, prediction or investment recommendation. Orbit has no backtested validation of predictive performance.

06

Keep control of your data

The app asks for no account, API key or wallet. Scripts, styles, logos and snapshots come from the same site. Your browser does not contact data providers; external links open only when you choose to follow them.

Your browser stores favorites, display preferences and language. There is no synchronization across devices. You can clear this data through your browser settings. Orbit adds no cookies or advertising trackers. The server may retain technical logs for operation and security; see the privacy notice.
